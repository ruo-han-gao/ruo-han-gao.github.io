Research

What I Study

I am interested in how social environments, emotional life, and brain structure shape each other across the lifespan.

Research

My research focuses on the associations between social environments and cerebellar structure in both young and older populations. I am interested in how social health affects, and is affected by, brain structure and function.

Current themes in my work include loneliness, emotional perception, emotional regulation, aging, and the ways social experience may become visible in the brain.

Questions

Social Environments

How do everyday social contexts relate to individual differences in brain structure and function?

Cerebellar Structure

How is cerebellar anatomy associated with social health in young and older populations?

Loneliness

How does loneliness connect with cognition, emotion, and neural outcomes?

Emotion

How do emotional perception and emotional regulation link social experience with the brain?
