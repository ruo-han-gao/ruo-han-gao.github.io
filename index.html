About

I am a PhD candidate at the Max Planck School of Cognition. Currently, I am part of the Lise Meitner Research Group Cognitive Neurogenetics at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig, Germany.

Before entering the field of cognition, I worked as a Chinese-English medical translator and completed a Master of Translation and Interpreting at Chongqing Medical University, China. Following my interest in the human mind, social behavior, and the brain, I later began a second academic path in Cognitive Science, completing an M.Sc. at RPTU University Kaiserslautern-Landau, Germany.

Changing academic fields was neither an easy decision nor an easy path, but it has been deeply worthwhile. If you are thinking about following an interest that seems far from where you started, I encourage you to give that curiosity a fair chance. I am always happy to chat with people who are considering an unconventional path of their own.
