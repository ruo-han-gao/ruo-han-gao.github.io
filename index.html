About

I am a PhD student at the Max Planck School of Cognition. I study the associations between social environments and cerebellar structure in both young and older populations.

My work focuses on how social health affects, and is affected by, brain structure and function. I am especially interested in the links between social experience, emotion, and cognition across the lifespan.

Research Interests

Social Environments

How social context and lived experience relate to individual differences in brain structure and function.

Cerebellar Structure

Associations between cerebellar anatomy and social health in young and older populations.

Loneliness

The role of loneliness as both a social experience and a factor connected to cognitive and neural outcomes.

Emotion

Emotional perception and emotional regulation as pathways linking social health, cognition, and the brain.
